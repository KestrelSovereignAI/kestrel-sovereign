This directory contains the foundational, high-level principles that govern the Kestrel project and all agents created within its framework. These documents are the philosophical and ethical bedrock of the system.

- **KESTREL_CONSTITUTION.md:** The supreme law of the Kestrel ecosystem. It defines the fundamental rights, responsibilities, and relationship between the human Sovereign and their AI Executor.
- **US_CONSTITUTION.md:** The full text of the Constitution of the United States. It serves as a primary source of inspiration and a foundational reference for the principles encoded in the Kestrel Constitution. It is also used as a document for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground the agent in established legal and ethical principles.
