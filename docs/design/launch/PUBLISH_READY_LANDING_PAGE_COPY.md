# Kestrel Landing Page Copy: Publish-Ready Draft

This draft is the tightened, publish-ready version of the earlier wireframe. It is written to be dropped directly into a launch page with minimal editing.

## Hero

### Eyebrow

The open foundation for Sovereign AI

### Headline

Own your AI.

### Subheadline

Kestrel is the open foundation for Sovereign AI: identity, memory, privacy, and governance that belong to the user, not the platform.

### Body

Most AI today is rented intelligence. The model may help you, but the durable layers still belong to someone else. Identity lives inside the provider. Memory lives inside the platform. Rules are hidden in product logic you cannot inspect or carry forward.

Kestrel gives developers a way to build agents differently: with portable identity, persistent memory, and constitutional governance enforced above the model.

## Section 1

### Heading

AI should not belong to the platform.

### Copy

As AI systems become more personal and more trusted, the most important layers stop being the model alone. Identity, memory, privacy, and governance become the real trust surface. Today, those layers are usually trapped inside a provider. Kestrel exists to separate them from the model so they can belong to the person or organization the system serves.

## Section 2

### Heading

Not another wrapper. The foundation layer.

### Copy

Kestrel is an open-source framework for building sovereign agents.

It is for teams that need continuity, accountability, and user ownership rather than another provider-controlled abstraction. If your system needs to persist beyond a single session, a single model, or a single vendor, this is the layer you build on.

The three durable layers of Sovereign AI

Portable identity

Every agent has a cryptographic identity that is not trapped inside one provider or platform.

### Card 2

Persistent memory you own

## Section 4

### Heading

What is open at launch

### Copy

Kestrel open-sources the sovereign agent framework itself: identity, memory, constitutional governance, and the runtime needed to run sovereign agents.

Clinical deployments and product-specific integrations are not the headline of the launch. They matter as proof that the system is real, not as the center of the message.

## Section 5

### Heading

This is not a thought experiment.

### Copy

Kestrel is already being used in a real clinical setting, where continuity, trust, and governance are not optional. That matters because this launch should not read like speculative architecture or a feature parade. It should read like infrastructure that has already had to survive contact with reality.

## Section 6

### Heading

Built for developers working on durable AI systems

### Copy

Kestrel is for builders creating AI systems that need continuity, accountability, and user ownership. It is not optimized to be another all-purpose consumer AI surface. It is built for agents that need to last, carry context forward, and remain legible to the people who rely on them.
