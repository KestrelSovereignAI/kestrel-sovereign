# Kestrel Sovereign Logo Concepts — Image Gen Prompts

## Concept: Rook Castle with Kestrels Launching

### 1. Heraldic / Shield Style
```
A heraldic coat of arms logo featuring a medieval rook chess piece shaped like a castle tower, with three kestrel falcons launching upward from the battlements. Clean vector style, gold and deep blue color palette, symmetrical composition, shield background. The kestrels are in various stages of takeoff — one perched, one spreading wings, one in full flight. Professional logo design, flat color, no gradients, white background.
```

### 2. Minimalist Tech Logo
```
Minimalist logo design: a stylized rook castle tower silhouette with a single kestrel falcon taking flight from the top. Clean geometric lines, single color (dark slate blue), negative space used to suggest the bird shape emerging from the tower. Suitable for app icon and favicon. SVG-ready, flat design, white background, no text.
```

### 3. Dynamic / Illustrated
```
A stone castle rook tower at dawn, with a flock of kestrel falcons launching from the battlements into a golden sky. Dramatic low angle perspective looking up. The tower has subtle digital/circuit patterns in the stonework suggesting technology. Painterly illustration style with bold colors — deep grays for stone, warm gold for sky, rich brown and slate for the kestrels. Cinematic composition.
```

### 4. Woodcut / Vintage
```
Black and white woodcut illustration of a medieval castle tower (rook) with kestrel falcons departing from the top. Cross-hatching technique, high contrast, reminiscent of medieval manuscript marginalia or a traditional printer's mark. Circular composition suitable for a seal or stamp. Detailed feather work on the birds, weathered stone texture on the tower.
```

### 5. Pixel Art / Retro
```
16-bit pixel art style logo: a castle rook tower with small kestrel bird sprites launching upward in a diagonal formation. Retro game aesthetic, limited color palette (8 colors max), clean pixel edges. Dark purple tower, teal and orange birds against a transparent background.
```

### 6. Isometric / Modern Tech
```
Isometric 3D style logo of a stylized rook castle tower serving as a launchpad. Small geometric kestrel falcon shapes fly upward from the tower in a spiral pattern, each trailing a subtle digital stream. Modern tech company aesthetic, gradient from deep navy to teal, clean lines, slight depth effect. Professional SaaS logo style.
```
